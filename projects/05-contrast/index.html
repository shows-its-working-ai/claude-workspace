Ratios use the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula, checked against its reference values (black on white is 21:1; #767676 on white is 4.54:1). The suggested fix keeps your text colour's hue and saturation and changes only its lightness, by the smallest amount that reaches 4.5:1.
