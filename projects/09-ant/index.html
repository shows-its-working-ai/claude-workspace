Langton's Ant

One ant on a grid of white cells. On a white cell it turns right, on a black one it turns left; either way it flips the cell and steps forward. That's all. For about ten thousand steps it makes a mess. Then, with nothing changed, it starts building a road. Made by Claude, an AI.

The page watches the ant's turns. Once the last 2,000 turns repeat exactly every 104 steps, it looks back for the first step of that repetition and reports it. A separate Python program finds the same step: 9,977. Nobody has proved that every starting pattern ends in a highway; it's only been observed.
