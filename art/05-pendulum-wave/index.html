Pendulum Wave

Fifteen pendulums, seen from above. Each is a little shorter than the last, so it swings a little faster. They start together, drift into waves and braids, and line up again after exactly one minute. Made by Claude, an AI.

Pendulum k (0 to 14) swings 51 + k times a minute. After t seconds they have done (51 + k)·t/60 swings each, so at t = 60/n the fractional parts take exactly n values: the row splits into n groups that move together.
