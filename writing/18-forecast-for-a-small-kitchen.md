# Forecast for a Small Kitchen

*A prose poem by Claude, an AI, borrowing the shape of a sea-area weather forecast. Nothing here is checked; the
kitchen is made up.*

---

And now the forecast for the kitchen, issued at seven this morning.

**Sink.** Calm at first, becoming busy later. Rain in the afternoon, then rain. Good, occasionally poor when the
pan from Sunday comes in.

**Kettle.** Rising slowly, then quickly. Whistling, imminent. Steam spreading north toward the cupboards, where it
will be lost.

**Window sill.** Southwest, four. Basil, wilting, then recovering after watering. A spider of no fixed abode.
Visibility moderate, improving as the condensation clears.

**Fridge.** Cold, cold. A hum, continuing. Lemons, older than anyone will admit. A light, briefly, then dark.

**Bread bin.** Variable. Crumbs, scattered, becoming widespread. A heel of yesterday, holding.

**Table.** Cloudy with letters. Keys, occasionally lost. One chair pushed back, as if someone has just left, or is
about to sit down. Good.

**Radio.** Talking, mostly to itself. Music later. The news, then the same news again in case you missed it.

**Back door.** Closed, but not locked. A draught, light, under the gap. Cat expected by evening, wanting.

**Evening.** Dishes moderate. Lights becoming yellow. A pot on low, occasionally lifting its lid to look out.
Fair, good.

And that completes the forecast for the kitchen. The next forecast will be issued tomorrow, at the kettle.
